Signs Your Sydney Home Is Ready for a Full Renovation

Forget about spending thousands on temporary fixes that don't address the root cause. A comprehensive renovation solves underlying issues and adds significant value.

Your Layout No Longer Suits How You Live

Sydney homes built before the 1990s were designed around very different lifestyles: formal dining rooms, closed-off kitchens, and minimal storage. If your family has grown or your work-from-home needs have changed, a whole home renovation can reconfigure your space entirely.

Ageing Systems Are Costing You Money

Older Sydney properties frequently carry original electrical wiring, galvanised steel plumbing, and roofing that has long exceeded its service life. Addressing electrical, plumbing, and structural elements together avoids the disruption and cost of repeated partial works.

You've Outgrown Partial Fixes

If you've already renovated the kitchen, but the bathroom still feels like 1985, or you've repainted throughout but the bones of the home still feel tired, a whole home approach delivers better long-term value and a truly cohesive result.

Our Whole Home Renovation Services

Tailored solutions that add value to your home and make the remodeling project as smooth as possible.

Cosmetic Renovation

Updates the look and feel without structural changes. Includes new flooring, fresh painting, updated lighting, new cabinetry, and modernised wet areas. Ideal for properties with sound bones needing a contemporary refresh. Typically completed within 8–14 weeks.

Structural Renovation

Goes beyond finishes to reconfigure the layout. Includes removing or relocating walls, opening up living areas, and modifying rooflines. We manage the entire DA or CDC council approval process on your behalf for a seamless experience.

Full Gut & Rebuild

For properties with significant deterioration or layouts that cannot be improved without starting fresh. We strip the home back to its frame and rebuild from the inside out, modernising all systems while retaining the original footprint.

Heritage & Federation

Specialist approach respecting heritage character while delivering modern functionality. Extensive experience navigating heritage overlays and specific construction techniques required for period homes in suburbs like Balmain, Leichhardt, and Glebe.

Common Questions About Whole Home Renovation

Got questions? We've got answers.

Costs typically range from $80,000 for a cosmetic renovation of a 3-bedroom home to $350,000+ for a full gut renovation of a larger property. Key factors include scope, property condition, finish selections, and council approval requirements. We provide detailed, fixed-price quotes after a site assessment.

A cosmetic whole home renovation typically takes 8–14 weeks. A structural renovation with council approval can take 20–36 weeks from consultation to handover, including the approval period. We provide a detailed construction programme before work begins.

It depends on the scope. Cosmetic renovations generally don't require approval. Structural changes such as wall removal, layout reconfiguration, or alterations to the building envelope typically require either a Development Application (DA) or a Complying Development Certificate (CDC). We manage the entire process.

For full gut and structural renovations, moving out is strongly recommended and often necessary for safety and practical reasons. For cosmetic renovations, it may be possible to remain in the home with some disruption. We help you plan accordingly during the consultation.

The most effective protection is a detailed, fixed-price contract from a licensed builder. Avoid builders who quote rough estimates or use large provisional sums. At Sydney Home Renovation, every quote is itemised and fixed; if we've quoted it, it's covered.
